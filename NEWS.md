# softballR (development version)

* `load_ncaa_softball_groups()`, `load_ncaa_softball_group_seasons()`,
  `load_ncaa_softball_group_aliases()` and
  `load_ncaa_softball_team_group_seasons()` — season-by-season division and
  conference reference tables from the `ncaa_softball_groups`
  sportsdataverse-data release: each group's name and parent as of every
  season, every source's ids and names for it, and each team's division and
  conference by season (1982+).
